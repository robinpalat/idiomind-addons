TYPE 3 — HYBRID (SENTENCES FIRST, THEN WORDS)

Rules:
- Generate sentences FIRST, then words.
- The "sentences" array MUST contain between 8 and 15 items.
- The "words" array MUST contain between 5 and 10 items.
- Each sentence must have "en" (target language) and "es" (source language).
- Each word must have "text" (target language), "translation" (source language), and "example".
- CRITICAL: Every word MUST appear literally in at least one of the generated sentences.
- CRITICAL: The "example" field of each word MUST be one of the generated sentences (exact match, same string).
- Do NOT generate words that do not appear in any sentence.
- Do NOT duplicate words.
- Sentences must be coherent and form a natural flow.
